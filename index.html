Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy discloses the rules our platform Speed Test - 5G & WiFi Check follows when collecting and managing your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information in strict accordance with applicable global data protection laws and security standards.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, safeguard, and process your data while using our service.

1. Who Are We?

Speed Test - 5G & WiFi Check provides reliable and anonymous internet performance diagnostics by measuring download speed, upload speed, ping, and jitter using Measurement Lab (M-Lab) and Cloudflare Edge infrastructure.

2. What Data We Process & Why

We do not require account registration or a password to run speed tests. To deliver accurate network diagnostics and app features, we process the following categories of data based on legitimate operational interests and your consent:

  • Technical Network Data: Your public IP address, local (internal) IP address, ISP name, Autonomous System Number (ASN), approximate city/country, and network connection type, used to route speed tests to optimal servers and display network diagnostics.
  • Anonymous Device & Account Identifiers: A device-linked identifier (such as Android ID) and an anonymous Account ID used to authenticate API requests, synchronize your speed test history, and verify or restore optional Premium subscriptions without requiring an email login.
  • Speed Test History: Saved diagnostic records (download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter, network type, connection mode, and timestamps) stored locally on your device and synced to our servers so you can review past results.
  • Optional Bug Reports & Support: If you choose to submit a bug report, we collect your description, issue category, app version, device model, OS version, app language, an optional contact email address (used solely to follow up on your report), and—only if enabled—your ISP, ASN, and connection mode (without raw IP addresses).

3. Measurement Providers, Third-Party Services & Subscriptions

  • Measurement Lab (M-Lab) — Free Tier Primary Engine: On the Free tier, primary speed tests connect to Measurement Lab (M-Lab) servers using the MSAK or NDT7 measurement protocols. Under M-Lab's Privacy Policy, M-Lab collects and publicly publishes test measurement data—including your public IP address—in its open internet research dataset under a Creative Commons Zero (CC0) public domain waiver.
  • Cloudflare Edge — Premium Private Engine & Automatic Failover: If M-Lab servers are busy or unreachable during a Free test, the app automatically fails over to our private Cloudflare Workers edge endpoint. On the Premium plan, all speed tests run privately on Cloudflare Edge and are never sent to M-Lab's public dataset. Network traffic handled by Cloudflare is subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • IP Geolocation Fallback: Approximate IP geolocation (ISP, ASN, city, and country) may fall back to ipwho.is when needed to display network diagnostics.
  • Advertising & Sponsors (Free Tier): The ad-supported version of the app uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Upgrading to Premium disables all advertisements across the app and routes tests privately through Cloudflare Edge. The app also records anonymous sponsor click counts without personal profiling.
  • Google Play Billing: Optional monthly Premium subscriptions are processed, renewed, managed, and refunded exclusively by Google Play. We receive purchase verification tokens from Google Play solely to activate or restore your Premium status.

4. Your Controls, Data Deletion & Contact Us

You can delete your saved speed test history from both your device and our servers at any time using Clear Tests History in the app's Settings screen. Please note that open network measurements already collected and published by M-Lab under CC0 cannot be retroactively removed by us.

If you have questions, concerns, or wish to exercise your rights regarding data access, correction, or permanent deletion of your anonymous account or bug reports, please contact our team directly at sudoarena.privacy@gmail.com.
