This Privacy Policy discloses the rules our platform Speed Test - 5G & WiFi Check follows when collecting and managing your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information in strict accordance with applicable global data protection laws and security standards.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, safeguard, and process your data while using our service.

1. Who Are We?

Speed Test - 5G & WiFi Check provides reliable and anonymous internet performance diagnostics by measuring download speed, upload speed, ping, and jitter across global Content Delivery Network (CDN) servers.

2. Why Do We Process Data?

The primary purpose of processing technical connection data is to execute accurate network speed tests, route your device to the nearest test server, and continuously optimize application stability and performance across devices.

We do not require account registration to run speed tests. Temporary network identifiers (such as your IP address) are processed solely to establish server connections and determine approximate network routing, and are handled based on legitimate operational interests and your consent.
