Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy discloses the rules our platform Speed Test - 5G & WiFi Check follows when collecting and managing your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information in strict accordance with applicable global data protection laws and security standards.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, safeguard, and process your data while using our service.

1. Who Are We?

Speed Test - 5G & WiFi Check provides reliable and anonymous internet performance diagnostics by measuring download speed, upload speed, TCP ping, and jitter across global Content Delivery Network (CDN) servers.

2. What Data We Process & Why

We do not require account registration or a password to run speed tests. To deliver accurate network diagnostics and app features, we process the following categories of data based on legitimate operational interests and your consent:

  • Technical Network Data: Your public IP address, local (private) IP address, ISP name, Autonomous System Number (ASN), approximate city/country, and network connection type, used to route speed tests to optimal servers and display network diagnostics.
  • Anonymous Device & Account Identifiers: A device-linked identifier (such as Android ID) and an anonymous Account ID used to authenticate API requests, synchronize your speed test history, and verify or restore optional Premium subscriptions without requiring an email login.
  • Speed Test History: Saved diagnostic records (download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter, network type, connection mode, and timestamps) stored locally on your device and synced to our servers so you can review past results.
  • Optional Bug Reports & Support: If you choose to submit a bug report, we collect your description, issue category, app version, device model, OS version, app language, an optional contact email address (used solely to follow up on your report), and—only if enabled—your ISP, ASN, and connection mode (without raw IP addresses).

3. Third-Party Services, Advertising & Subscriptions

  • Speed Test & Geolocation Providers: Throughput and latency tests connect directly to global CDN servers operated by Cloudflare and CacheFly. Approximate IP geolocation may fall back to ipwho.is.
  • Advertising & Sponsors (Free Tier): The ad-supported version of the app uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Upgrading to Premium disables all advertisements across the app. The app also records anonymous sponsor click counts without personal profiling.
  • Google Play Billing: Optional monthly Premium subscriptions are processed, renewed, managed, and refunded exclusively by Google Play. We receive purchase verification tokens from Google Play solely to activate or restore your Premium status.

4. Your Controls, Data Deletion & Contact Us

You can delete your saved speed test history from both your device and our servers at any time using Clear Tests History in the app's Settings screen.

If you have questions, concerns, or wish to exercise your rights regarding data access, correction, or permanent deletion of your anonymous account or bug reports, please contact our team directly at sudoarena.privacy@gmail.com.
